ACME INDUSTRIAL CORP (ACME) — Q4 2025 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT
January 28, 2026

PARTICIPANTS:
- Robert Zhang, CEO
- Maria Torres, CFO
- David Park, VP Investor Relations

PREPARED REMARKS:

David Park: Good morning and thank you for joining the Acme Industrial fourth quarter 2025 earnings call. Please note that today's discussion contains forward-looking statements.

Robert Zhang: Thank you, David. Q4 was a strong finish to a transformative year. Let me share the highlights.

Revenue for Q4 came in at $4.2 billion, up 18% year-over-year and above our guidance range of $3.9 to $4.1 billion. For the full year, revenue was $15.8 billion, representing 22% growth.

Our gross margin expanded to 42.3% in Q4, up from 38.7% in the prior year quarter, driven by our shift toward higher-margin recurring software revenue, which now represents 34% of total revenue versus 26% a year ago.

Operating income was $890 million in Q4, with an operating margin of 21.2%. Adjusted EBITDA reached $1.1 billion, representing a 26.2% margin.

We added 340 new enterprise customers in the quarter, bringing our total to over 4,200. Net revenue retention was 128%, reflecting strong expansion within our existing customer base.

Maria Torres: Thank you, Robert. Let me walk through the details.

Free cash flow was $780 million in Q4 and $2.4 billion for the full year. We ended the quarter with $3.2 billion in cash and equivalents and total debt of $1.8 billion.

R&D spending was $520 million in Q4, or 12.4% of revenue, as we continue to invest in our AI-powered industrial automation platform.

For Q1 2026 guidance, we expect revenue of $4.0 to $4.2 billion, gross margin of 41 to 43%, and adjusted EBITDA margin of 24 to 26%.

For the full year 2026, we are raising our outlook. We now expect revenue of $18.5 to $19.5 billion, representing growth of 17 to 23%.

We see some headwinds from foreign exchange, particularly the weakening euro, which we estimate will reduce full-year revenue by approximately $200 million. We are also monitoring potential tariff impacts on our supply chain components sourced from Southeast Asia.

Robert Zhang: Looking ahead, we remain confident in our long-term targets of 20%+ revenue growth and 30% EBITDA margins by 2028. Our pipeline is the strongest it has ever been.
